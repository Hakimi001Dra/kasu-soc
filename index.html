About the Department

Rooted in Kaduna.
Engaged with the World.

The Department of Sociology at Kaduna State University is one of the foremost centres for sociological research and teaching in North-Central Nigeria.

Our research programmes address the most pressing social challenges in Kaduna State and Nigeria at large.

📋 Editorial Policy & Scope

›
The KASU Journal of Sociology & Social Sciences (KJSSS) publishes original research in all areas of sociology. Articles should be 6,000–9,000 words including references, formatted in APA 7th edition.

For Researchers

Submit Your Research

We welcome original research from Nigerian and international scholars.

  1. Prepare your manuscript according to the KJSSS Author Guidelines
  2. Remove all author-identifying information for blind review
  3. Submit via the online portal or email the editorial office directly
  4. Peer review process (8–12 weeks average turnaround)
  5. Revisions, acceptance, and publication in next available issue
