About the Department

Rooted in Kaduna.
Engaged with the World.

The Department of Sociology at Kaduna State University is one of the foremost centres for sociological research and teaching in North-Central Nigeria. We combine rigorous scholarship with deep community engagement.

Our research programmes address the most pressing social challenges in Kaduna State and Nigeria at large — from inter-ethnic relations and rural development to urban poverty, health inequalities, and climate-induced displacement.

📚
Peer-Reviewed Research

All publications undergo rigorous double-blind review by expert panels drawn from Nigerian and international universities.

🎓
Academic Programmes

B.Sc., M.Sc., and Ph.D. in Sociology, with specialisations in development, criminology, and health sociology.

🤝
Community Partnerships

Collaborative research with state ministries, NGOs, and communities across Kaduna State.

📋 Editorial Policy & Scope

›
The KASU Journal of Sociology & Social Sciences (KJSSS) publishes original research in all areas of sociology and cognate social science disciplines. The journal welcomes empirical, theoretical, and policy-oriented contributions relevant to Nigerian and African societies. Articles should be 6,000–9,000 words including references, formatted in APA 7th edition.

For Researchers

Submit Your Research

We welcome original research from Nigerian and international scholars. All submissions are double-blind peer reviewed and decisions communicated within 8 weeks.

  1. Prepare your manuscript according to the KJSSS Author Guidelines (APA 7th edition, 6,000–9,000 words)
  2. Remove all author-identifying information for blind review
  3. Submit via the online portal or email the editorial office directly
  4. Receive acknowledgement and your manuscript reference number
  5. Peer review process (8–12 weeks average turnaround)
  6. Revisions, acceptance, and publication in next available issue
